Merit, open merit and reserved seats in one table

What the words on a Pakistani merit list mean
TermWhat it meansExample
MeritYour position by aggregate: everyone who applied is ranked from the highest aggregate down.UHS publishes an order of merit with a merit number for every MDCAT candidate.
Open meritSeats that any eligible applicant can win on aggregate alone, with no quota, domicile district or family condition.UET A1 open merit, UHS open merit, KU category K open merit.
Reserved (quota) seatsA fixed number of seats kept for one group, filled on a separate list among that group only.UHS seats for disabled students, under-developed districts, Overseas Pakistanis; UET categories L, M, N, NM, O, S, T.
Self-finance / partly subsidizedSeats with a higher fee and their own merit, usually closing lower than open merit.UET A2 (partly subsidized fee), KU self-finance admissions.
Closing meritThe aggregate of the last student admitted in a list, for one program and category.KEMU closed at 94.87% on open merit in the final 2025 UHS list.

What is open merit?

Open merit means the seat goes to whoever has the higher aggregate, full stop. You still have to meet the basic eligibility (for example a Punjab domicile, at least 60% in FSc and at least 55% in MDCAT for a UHS MBBS seat), but nothing else about you matters. Most seats in public universities are open merit seats, so the open merit closing figure is the number most students should compare their aggregate with. Every closing merit on Apna Merit is open merit unless the table says otherwise.

What is the difference between merit and open merit?

"Merit" is the ranking itself; "open merit" is one group of seats filled from that ranking. A university first ranks everyone by aggregate (the merit), then fills each group of seats from the top of the ranking: open merit seats from everyone, and each reserved quota only from candidates who qualify for it. So the same student can be below the open merit closing figure and still get a reserved seat, or the other way round.

Real figures from UET Lahore's Fall 2026 4th merit list (main campus): Mechanical Engineering closed at 82.28% on A1 open merit, 80.62% on A2 (partly subsidized fee) and 68.77% for children of UET employees (category M). Computer Science closed at 85.37% on A1 open merit and 83.10% on A2. The UET merit list has every category.

Open merit at UHS, UET, KU and PU

  • UHS (MBBS/BDS Punjab): open merit seats are filled from the UHS order of merit; reserved seats (students with disabilities, under-developed districts, Cholistan, Overseas Pakistanis and dual nationals, other provinces) have separate lists. See the MDCAT merit list for open merit closing merit by college.
  • UET Lahore: A1 is open merit for Punjab domicile with the subsidized fee, A2 is open merit with a partly subsidized fee, and the -Med versions are for FSc Pre-Medical students; letters such as L, M, N, O and S are reserved categories (prospectus pp. 22-25).
  • University of Karachi: open merit departments admit in categories K (Karachi institutions), S (rest of Sindh) and P (outside Sindh); K gets preference. See the KU merit list.
  • Punjab University: the published "merit of last year" figures are the open merit of each program and shift; teacher and employee quotas have their own merit. See the PU merit list.

Which figure should you compare with?

Compare your aggregate with the open merit closing merit of the last list for your program, unless you are sure you qualify for a quota. Quota seats are few, and a quota list can close higher or lower than open merit in different years. Work out your aggregate with the aggregate calculator for your university, then check it with the merit predictor. To understand closing merit itself, read what closing merit means.

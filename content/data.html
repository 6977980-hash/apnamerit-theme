Every aggregate formula and closing merit on Apna Merit is published as open data. Each JSON file is the same file that builds the pages, with an official source_url on every record. You may reuse it, including commercially, under CC BY 4.0: please credit "Apna Merit (apnamerit.com)" with a link.

[am_data_catalog]

What is in a file?

  • formulas: each aggregate formula with its parts (Matric, FSc, test), weights in percent, usual total marks and the official source.
  • merit: one record per program, campus, shift, year, merit list and category, with closing (the aggregate of the last admitted candidate, in percent) and source_url.
  • defaults: the list and category our pages show first.

How we collect and check the figures is explained in our methodology; the original documents are listed on the data sources page. The files allow cross-origin requests, so you can load them straight from a web page. Prefer a ready-made widget? See embed a calculator. For a written summary of the figures, read the merit data report 2026.

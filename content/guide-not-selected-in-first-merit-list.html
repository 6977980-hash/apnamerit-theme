Why the first list is not the end

UHS fills public MBBS and BDS seats in rounds. The first college-wise selection list (17 November 2026 for MBBS) places students on all open and reserved seats by merit and preference. Then students who got a lower-preference college are upgraded as higher ones free up, students who do not pay or join lose their seat, and the vacancies go to the next applicants in merit. The policy says admissions close only after three or more lists; in 2025 the public MBBS cycle ran to a 13th list.

The pattern is the same every year: the closing merit of a public college moves by a fraction of a point between the first and the last list, because almost every selected student joins. Private colleges move much more; in 2025 some private MBBS colleges closed 10 to 15 points lower in the final list than in the first.

If you were selected but want a better college

In public colleges upgradation is automatic and mandatory: pay the fee and join the allotted college within the deadline, and if a higher-preference college opens up in a later list you are moved there and must report to the new college in time, or the admission is cancelled. You cannot opt out or pick a college you left off your preference list. In private colleges a student may, at joining, choose to forgo upgradation; that choice is final.

Other routes this year

  • Private MBBS/BDS in Punjab: same UHS portal, separate preference list with no limit on colleges; Rs 600,000 deposit within three working days of selection, the rest paid to the college. In the final 2025 list private MBBS closed between 61.00% and 90.84% on open merit (full table).
  • NUMS: Army Medical College and affiliated colleges admit through their own process; see the NUMS merit list.
  • BDS: the public BDS portal opens 15 to 30 December 2026 with its own lists from 11 January 2027; in 2025 public BDS closed between 93.42% and 93.46%.
  • Next year: MDCAT 2026 remains valid for 2027-28 and 2028-29 admissions, so a better FSc result or a new MDCAT attempt both help.

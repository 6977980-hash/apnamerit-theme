Which subjects count?

  • O level (Matric equivalence), exams taken in Pakistan: eight subjects: English, Mathematics, Urdu, Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies, plus three electives, each with at least grade E. For the science group the electives must include Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Computer Science.
  • O level taken abroad: five subjects: English, Mathematics and three electives.
  • A level (FSc equivalence): three main subjects with at least grade E: Physics, Chemistry and Biology for pre-medical, or Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for pre-engineering, on top of the O level requirements.

Rules: IBCC, British System (O and A levels).

Using your equivalence for admission

Universities use your IBCC percentage in place of Matric or FSc marks. Enter it in the right calculator: MDCAT, NUST (which has its own A level formula), Punjab University or any other university. For plain Matric and FSc marks, use the percentage calculator.

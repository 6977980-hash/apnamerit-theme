Headline figures

  • Karachi University's test-based closing percentages (candidates with at least 50 in the entrance test) rose by 1.69 points on average from 2025 to 2026, across 26 programmes listed in both years. Pharm-D (morning) closed highest at 84.27%.
  • Punjab's public MBBS colleges closed 0.85 points lower on average in the final 2025 UHS list than in the last 2024 list. King Edward Medical University was still highest at 94.87%, 1.19 points below 2024.

  • UET Taxila's newest official closing positions (2024 entry) ran from 79.44% for Software Engineering to 53.07% for Environmental Engineering.

How the figures are counted

Closing merit is the aggregate of the last candidate admitted to a program in a merit list (see what closing merit means). We copy it only from official university documents, never from third-party estimates, and record the year, list, category, campus and shift with each figure. The full rules are in our methodology.

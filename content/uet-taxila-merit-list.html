UET Taxila closing merit 2024 at a glance

  • Highest: Software Engineering 79.44%, then BS Computer Science (pre-medical) 78.18% and Computer Science 77.98%.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 70.82%, well below the computing programs.
  • Core engineering closed between 58% and 66%: Civil 65.52%, Mechatronics 63.56%, Electronics 62.33%, Telecom 59.55%, Mechanical 59.01%, Electrical 58.06%.
  • Lowest: Industrial & Manufacturing 53.78% and Environmental 53.07%.

What aggregate is needed for UET Taxila?

If your aggregate is above a program's 2024 closing figure, you were in range for it last year; within about 1.5 points either side is borderline (our method). Calculate yours with the UET Taxila aggregate calculator (33% ECAT, 50% FSc Part-I, 17% Matric), which also lists the programs your aggregate cleared.

Other categories

The same prospectus table also gives closing figures for reserved categories (for example disabled persons, backward districts of Punjab, children of graduate engineers, Tehsil Taxila, partial-subsidized and overseas Pakistanis). They are often lower than open merit but cover very few seats. We show open merit only; see page 136 of the prospectus for the rest.

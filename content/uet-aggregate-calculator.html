How is the UET Lahore aggregate calculated?

UET Lahore ranks applicants to ECAT-based programs by a weighted percentage set on page 20 of its Undergraduate Prospectus Fall 2026 (the same weights as the Spring 2026 prospectus): entrance test (ECAT) 33% + first-year Intermediate (FSc Part-I) 50% + Matric 17%.

  • ECAT share = ECAT marks ÷ 400 × 33
  • FSc share = FSc Part-I marks ÷ Part-I total × 50
  • Matric share = Matric marks ÷ 1100 × 17

Non-ECAT programs such as BBA and BBIT use 75% first-year Intermediate + 25% Matric (pick that formula above). Diploma (DAE) holders use 33% ECAT + 67% for first and second year together, which this calculator does not cover. The minimum aggregate for admission is 50%.

What aggregate do you need for UET Lahore?

In the Fall 2026 4th Merit List (A1 open merit, main campus), Cybersecurity closed at 85.60%, Computer Science at 85.37%, Electrical at 83.33%, Mechanical at 82.28% and Computer Engineering at 82.11%; every main-campus engineering program closed above 70%. Sub-campuses closed lower: Computer Science 79.75% at KSK, 75.79% at Faisalabad, 72.70% at Narowal and 71.46% at RCET Gujranwala. Merit rose for almost every program from Fall 2025. These are last-admitted figures; this year's merit depends on this year's applicants and seats.

What about the interview formula?

UET's old admission portal also showed a "Merit Calculation" image with 20% entry test, 70% academics and 10% interview. It matched the interview-based Spring 2025 admission notice. The Spring 2026 and Fall 2026 prospectuses give the 33/50/17 formula with no interview, so the calculator uses that. If UET announces a different formula for your session, follow UET's notice.

Hafiz-e-Quran and sports marks

A Hafiz-e-Quran can get 0 to 20 extra marks and a sportsperson up to 10 marks added to Intermediate marks, both decided by a UET verification committee after a test. Because the committee sets the number, the calculator leaves them out.

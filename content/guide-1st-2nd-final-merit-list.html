What is the difference between the 1st, 2nd and final merit list?

Universities fill seats in rounds. The 1st merit list offers seats to the top applicants for each program. Some of them join, some move to a program or college they ranked higher, and some leave for another university. Their empty seats go to the next students in merit in the 2nd list, and the process repeats until seats are full. The final list is the last round, and its last admitted aggregate is the program's closing merit.

Because each round reaches further down the merit order, the closing aggregate almost never rises from one list to the next. How far it falls depends on how many students leave.

Why does merit drop more in some programs?

  • Demand at the top. Top public colleges and programs are almost everyone's first choice, so few students leave and the merit barely moves.
  • Fee and preference. Private seats are expensive and many students admitted there move to a public seat or decline, so private merit falls a lot.
  • Number of rounds. More lists mean the final list reaches further down.
  • Upgrades. When one student moves up to a preferred program, a chain of seats opens below.

Engineering admissions work the same way, and the gap between programs is large: at UET Taxila in 2024, Software Engineering closed at 79.44% and Environmental Engineering at 53.07%. See the UET Taxila merit list for the spread across programs.

Which list should you compare your aggregate with?

Use the final list of last year as your main guide, because it shows the lowest aggregate that still got a seat. Then look at the 1st list: if you are above last year's 1st-list figure you are very likely in; if you are only above the final figure, you may get in only in a later list. Our calculators, such as the MDCAT aggregate calculator and the UET Taxila aggregate calculator, compare you with the final list for this reason.

Should you accept a seat in the 1st list?

Usually yes. UHS, for example, upgrades admitted students to a higher-choice college in later lists (its lists have an "Upgraded To" column), and UET Peshawar's prospectus says provisionally admitted students are moved to higher choices on merit when seats fall vacant. If you do not complete admission by the deadline, you can lose the seat. Read the official notice of each list for its fee deadline and upgrade rules.

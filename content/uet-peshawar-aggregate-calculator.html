How is the UET Peshawar aggregate calculated?

UET Peshawar's UG Prospectus 2026-27 (Admission Rules, "Determination of Merit" 6.1) sets the BSc Engineering merit as entrance test 50% + Intermediate Part-I (adjusted marks) 40% + SSC 10%.

  • Test share = entrance test marks ÷ 100 × 50
  • FSc share = Part-I marks ÷ Part-I total × 40
  • Matric share = SSC marks ÷ SSC total × 10

The 2026-27 engineering entrance test has 100 MCQs (Physics 40, Mathematics 35, English 15, Chemistry or Computer Science 10), one mark each and no negative marking, so the calculator uses 100 as the test total. Pre-medical candidates sit a 65-MCQ paper whose 65 marks count as 100%: enter your marks out of 65 by changing the total.

BS Computer Science has no entrance test: 40% SSC + 60% Intermediate Part-I (prospectus, BS programmes 6.1). Pick it in the formula box.

Adjusted marks, Hafiz-e-Quran and DAE

"Adjusted marks" means UET Peshawar deducts marks for repeated or improved papers (rule 6.3, at most 20 marks), and candidates who take Mathematics as an additional subject lose 10 marks. A Hafiz-e-Quran candidate who passes the university's Hifz test gets an additional 20 marks. DAE holders are judged on their 1st and 2nd year diploma marks for the 40% part.

Where is the UET Peshawar closing merit?

UET Peshawar displays revised merit lists on the main campus notice board, and we have not found an official program-wise closing merit online yet. We will add a UET Peshawar merit list only from an official document; until then, compare your aggregate with UET Taxila closing merits as a rough guide.

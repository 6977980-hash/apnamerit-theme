Information Technology University (ITU) Lahore does not print a closing merit. It posts each BS merit list as a table of the candidates offered admission, each with a MERIT score, on its Fall 2025 merit-list pages. The figures below are the lowest MERIT score in the latest list we found for each program, read on 1 November 2026, so they are derived from ITU's own lists, not a number ITU published. Where ITU ran a second admission round, the round-1 figure is under the list "Round 1 last list".

ITU closing merit 2025 at a glance

  • Highest: BS Software Engineering 83.05% (3rd list), BS Computer Science 81.27% (2nd list) and BS Artificial Intelligence 80.81% (3rd list).
  • BS Computer Engineering: 77% in the 2nd list.
  • Business and economics programs: 53.54% to 63.69%, with Electrical Engineering at about 60% in both rounds.

How is the ITU merit calculated?

For BS Computer Science and BS Artificial Intelligence, ITU's Norms & Criteria give 15% Matric + 35% Intermediate + 50% ITU admission test (or SAT-I / USAT). Work yours out with the ITU aggregate calculator, which compares it with the CS and AI figures above. Other programs use a single academic-record weight that ITU does not split, so the calculator does not compare them.

Is this the final ITU merit?

Not necessarily. ITU does not say which list is the last, and each list names only the new offers of that round, so a later list could have closed lower. Treat these figures as a guide. For Lahore computing seats with an official closing figure, see PUCIT on the PU merit list and the UET Lahore merit list.

KEMU vs AIMC: the example above

In the final 2025 UHS list, King Edward Medical University closed at 94.87% and Allama Iqbal Medical College at 94.45% on open merit, 0.42 points apart, which is about 1.5 MDCAT marks with the PM&DC formula. In 2024 the two closed at 96.06% and 95.60%. The gap between the two oldest Lahore colleges stayed under half a point both years.

How to read a comparison

  • Same university: the figures use one formula, so the difference is real. For UHS colleges, one MDCAT mark is worth 0.28 aggregate points.
  • Different universities: each has its own formula (MDCAT, ECAT, academic only), so 85% at UET and 85% at PU are not the same thing. Use the comparison for the trend of each program, not to rank them against each other.
  • Which list: each figure is the university's main view (the final or last regular list, open merit). Where a year only has another list, its name is shown under the figure.

Where the figures come from

Every closing merit is copied from the university's own merit list or prospectus and links to it. The full tables are on the merit list pages, and the same data is free to download from open data.

Reporting a mistake

Please include:

  • the address of the page with the mistake,
  • what is wrong (for example, the program, year and list), and
  • a link to the official document that shows the correct figure, if you have it.

We check every report and correct confirmed errors within 48 hours, as described in our editorial policy.

What we cannot help with

We are not a university or an admission office. We cannot change your admission status, accept applications or tell you your official merit position. For those, contact the university's admission office directly; official websites are listed on our data sources page.

Please do not send us your CNIC number, roll number or other personal documents.

Run an academy, a student blog or a school website? Add a free Apna Merit aggregate calculator or the merit predictor to your page. The widget uses the same official formulas and closing merits as this site and updates by itself whenever a new merit list is added. No sign-up, no ads, no cookies.

[am_embed_builder]

How to add the widget

  1. Choose a widget above: one university's aggregate calculator or the merit predictor.
  2. Copy the code and paste it into your page as HTML (WordPress: a "Custom HTML" block; Blogger: HTML view).
  3. Publish. The widget sets its own height as students use it.

Please keep the link under the widget: it tells your readers where the numbers come from and how to check them. All figures come from official sources listed on our data sources page.

Other ways to use our data

Link to any calculator, for example the MDCAT aggregate calculator or the PU merit list, or download the tables from our open data page under CC BY 4.0.

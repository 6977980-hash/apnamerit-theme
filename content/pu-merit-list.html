PU closing merit 2025 at a glance

  • Highest: Pharm-D 91.34% (morning), then PUCIT Computer Science 87.18% (New Campus) and Data Science 86.1%.
  • Business: BBIT 81.83% and BBA at IBA 81.79%; Accounting & Finance at Hailey College of Commerce 78.45%.
  • Sciences: Microbiology & Molecular Genetics 82.54%, Biotechnology 79.32%, Physics 76.37%, Chemistry 72.08%, Mathematics 59.65%.
  • Engineering: Electrical 76.69%, Chemical (Petroleum & Gas) 76.20%, Chemical 72.89%.
  • Social sciences and arts: Political Science 79.49%, Clinical Psychology 79.43%, English Literature 77.73%, Journalism 70%.
  • Evening and self-supporting seats usually close 5 to 25 points below morning seats in the same department.

What aggregate is needed for Punjab University?

If your merit is above a program's 2025 figure, you would have been in range last year; within about 1.5 points either side is borderline (see our method). Work out your merit with the PU aggregate calculator: Matric ÷ 4 plus FA/FSc, out of 1375 with the usual totals.

Notes on the official list

  • PU's admission portal also shows candidate-wise merit lists for each program; we use the university's summary document, not individual names.
  • Some cells in the PDF give marks instead of a percentage (Pakistan Studies), "Nil" or "new program"; those are not in the table. Sheikh Zayed Islamic Centre gives separate boys and girls figures; see the PDF.
  • Law (LLB) figures assume the mandatory LAT/entry test is passed. College of Art & Design programs also need a drawing/aptitude test.
  • PU teacher and PU employee quotas have their own merit; the figures here are the published program merit.

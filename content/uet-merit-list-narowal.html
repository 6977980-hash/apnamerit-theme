Which list is the closing merit?

The Fourth Merit List is UET Lahore's last regular list and covers every program, so the table opens on it. Walk-in admissions and the two change-of-preference lists came later and only filled seats left open; pick them in the Merit list filter. UET's websites were down (error 522) when we built this page, so the official PDF was read from its web.archive.org copy.

What aggregate is needed for UET Narowal?

UET Lahore uses one aggregate for all its campuses: 33% ECAT, 50% FSc Part-I and 17% Matric. Calculate yours with the UET aggregate calculator and compare it with the figures above; within about 1.5 points either side is borderline (our method). Other campuses: UET KSK, UET Faisalabad, UET RCET Gujranwala.

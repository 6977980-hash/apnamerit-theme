Why does my aggregate differ between universities?

Each university gives different weights to Matric, FSc (or FSc Part-I) and its own entry test. The same marks can give 80% for one university and 75% for another, so always compare an aggregate with the closing merit of the same university. For example, MDCAT counts 50% for MBBS while NUST NET counts 75% for engineering.

What aggregate is needed for UET KSK?

UET Lahore uses one aggregate for all its campuses: 33% ECAT, 50% FSc Part-I and 17% Matric. Calculate yours with the UET aggregate calculator and compare it with the figures above; within about 1.5 points either side is borderline (our method). Other campuses: UET Faisalabad, UET Narowal, UET RCET Gujranwala.

How is the Bahria aggregate calculated?

Bahria University's official Admissions Policy (section 2.11.3) sets one formula for all undergraduate programs: admission test 60%, HSSC (FSc) 30% and SSC (Matric) 10%. The test can be Bahria's own computer-based test (BUET/CBT) or an accepted ETS score. If you take more than one test, Bahria counts the best score.

Bahria admission test passing marks and eligibility

  • Undergraduate admission test passing marks: 33% (policy section 2.10).
  • Minimum HSSC: 60% in FSc Pre-Engineering for engineering, 60% in FSc Pre-Medical for medical sciences, 50% for all other undergraduate programs.
  • MBBS and BDS at Bahria's medical and dental colleges do not use this formula; they follow the PM&DC formula (50% MDCAT, 40% FSc, 10% Matric). Use the MDCAT aggregate calculator for them.

Bahria merit lists and closing merit

Bahria announces merit lists campus-wise (for example Islamabad E-8 and H-11, Karachi and the health sciences campuses) but does not publish a closing-merit summary we could verify, so this page shows the formula only.

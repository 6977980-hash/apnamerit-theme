How to read the KU closing percentage

For test-based programmes KU first sets a minimum entrance test score (usually 50 out of 100), then admits candidates who passed it in order of their academic percentage. The closing figure is the academic percentage of the last candidate admitted. Some programmes also show a second row with a lower test threshold (for example 40 or 35), with its own academic closing percentage. By default the table shows the test score ≥ 50 rows; use the Category filter for the others and for Visual Studies.

Visual Studies: closing final points

The Department of Visual Studies ranks on final points = 20% academic + 60% aptitude test + 20% interview (test pass mark 50/100, interview 10/20). Fine Art closed at 60.13%, Architecture 57.92% and Product Design 50.33%. Select "Visual Studies final points" in the Category filter to see all eight.

What about KU's open-merit departments?

Most KU BS departments (Mathematics, Physics, English, Economics, Psychology and others) admit on open merit without an entrance test; KU announced their lists separately from 11 October. Those department-wise figures are not in the two closing documents above, so they are not in this table yet. We will add them when KU publishes a closing document for them.

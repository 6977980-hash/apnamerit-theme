The Year column gives the year the list was made, so you can compare the two rounds.

KU names each cycle by the year the batch starts, so the lists published in October 2026 are "Admissions 2027"; this page uses the year the list came out.

How to read the KU closing percentage

For test-based programmes KU first sets a minimum entrance test score (usually 50 out of 100), then admits candidates who passed it in order of their academic percentage. The closing figure is the academic percentage of the last candidate admitted; the test score is not added (work out yours with the KU merit calculator). Some programmes also show a second row with a lower test threshold (for example 40 or 35), with its own academic closing percentage. By default the table shows the latest (Admissions 2027) test score ≥ 50 rows; use the Year and Category filters for the previous round, the other thresholds and Visual Studies.

Visual Studies: closing final points

The Department of Visual Studies ranks on final points = 20% academic + 60% aptitude test + 20% interview (test pass mark 50/100, interview 10/20). Fine Art closed at 60.13%, Architecture 57.92% and Product Design 50.33%. Select "Visual Studies final points" in the Category filter to see all eight.

KU open merit closing percentage 2026 (department-wise)

Most KU BS departments (Mathematics, Physics, English, Economics, Psychology and others) admit on open merit, without an entrance test, in three categories: K (Matric, Intermediate or graduation from institutions in Karachi), S (rest of Sindh) and P (outside Sindh). K candidates get preference; S and P are considered only for seats left over. The table shows the category K closing percentages of the morning programme from KU's open merit closing list for Admissions 2027 (1 November 2026); pick the year 2025 for Admissions 2026 (Prospectus 2027, Appendix G). A figure of 45.00% or 50.00% usually means every eligible applicant was admitted.

KU morning vs evening closing percentage

KU runs a separate evening programme in some departments, with its own seats, fee and merit list, published after the morning lists. Evening seats close well below morning seats. In the evening lists of Admissions 2026 (dated 9 December 2025, reprinted in KU's last year closing and fee guidance, p. 5):

  • BS Computer Science (evening): 68.82% with at least 50 in the entrance test, against 77.91% in the morning the same year; Software Engineering 62.18% (morning 77.18%).
  • BBA (Karachi University Business School): 66.82% in the evening on open merit; the morning BBA is test-based (73.09% in 2025).
  • Pharm-D: 77.36% evening vs 82.45% morning in 2025, and 80.00% vs 84.27% in 2026.
  • Other evening programmes, category K: Digital Media Marketing 62.91%, Food Science & Technology 59.45%, Public Administration 50.45%, Commerce 50.27%, Mass Communication 47.55%; many others closed at the 45% minimum.

Choose "Evening" in the Shift filter of the tables above to see every evening row. KU had not published the Admissions 2027 evening lists on 12 November 2026; they usually follow in December.

MDCAT marks needed for each type of college

The table works backwards from the official closing merit of the last UHS list of 2025 (open merit) to the MDCAT score that would have reached it with the PM&DC formula (50% MDCAT, 40% FSc, 10% Matric), for three levels of FSc and Matric marks. "Pass mark" means the closing merit was so low that the MDCAT minimum (99/180 for MBBS, 90/180 for BDS) was the real limit; "not reachable" means even 180/180 was not enough with those FSc and Matric marks.

What the table shows

  • Government MBBS: with 1000/1100 in both FSc and Matric, a public medical college needed about 173 to 178 out of 180 in MDCAT in 2025. With 900/1100 no public seat was reachable at all, because FSc and Matric carry half of the aggregate.
  • Public BDS closed almost at the same level as public MBBS in 2025, so it needed about the same MDCAT score.
  • Private MBBS: from about 164/180 (Al-Aleem, Lahore) down to the 99/180 pass mark at the lowest private colleges, for the same FSc and Matric.
  • Private BDS: from about 100/180 at the top college down to the 90/180 BDS pass mark.

Every college has its own page with the MDCAT marks it needed: for example KEMU, Narowal Medical College, Al-Aleem or Sahara Medical College. All public and private colleges are on the MDCAT merit list.

Passing MDCAT is not the same as getting a seat

The MDCAT passing marks (55% for MBBS, 50% for BDS, with at least 60% in FSc) only make you eligible to apply. A seat depends on your aggregate against everyone else's: in 2025 the lowest government MBBS college still closed at 93.51%. Merit moves with each year's paper, so treat these figures as a guide.

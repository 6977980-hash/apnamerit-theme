Who can apply for MBBS and BDS in Punjab?

The UHS admission notice for session 2026-2027 lists four basic conditions for public-sector medical and dental colleges:

  1. MDCAT: passed MDCAT 2026, 2025 or 2024 with at least 55% marks for MBBS and 50% for BDS.
  2. FSc / HSSC: passed HSSC (pre-medical) or an equivalent 12th-grade qualification recognised by IBCC, with at least 60% marks, including Biology, Chemistry and Physics or Mathematics.
  3. Domicile: a domicile certificate from any district of Punjab, except for overseas Pakistanis and dual nationals applying on their reserved seats.
  4. Quota seats: candidates for quota or reserved seats must also meet that category's extra conditions in the prospectus.

The national rule behind this is regulation 5 of the PM&DC Admissions, Curriculum and Conduct Policy and Regulations 2026: an open-merit candidate anywhere in Pakistan must have passed MDCAT, scored at least 60% in HSSC or an equivalent 12th-grade qualification (or a higher percentage if PM&DC notifies one), and passed Biology and Chemistry, with Physics or Mathematics as the third subject.

Example: is 98 out of 180 enough?

98 ÷ 180 × 100 = 54.44%. That is below the 55% MBBS pass mark but above the 50% BDS pass mark, so with at least 60% in FSc this student can apply for BDS but not for MBBS. One more mark (99/180 = 55.00%) would meet the MBBS minimum. For FSc out of 1100, 660 marks is exactly 60%; out of 1200 you need 720.

Passing MDCAT is not the same as getting a seat

The pass marks only decide who may apply. Seats then go by aggregate (50% MDCAT, 40% FSc, 10% Matric). In the final 2025 UHS lists, Punjab public MBBS colleges closed between 93.51% and 94.87% on open merit and private MBBS colleges between 90.84% and 61.00%. A student with exactly the minimum marks (55% MDCAT and 60% FSc) has an aggregate of about 51.5% plus their Matric share, far below those figures. See the full MDCAT merit list and the UHS admission schedule.

What about the 52% and 47% pass marks?

On 8 April 2026 PM&DC let admitting universities make a one-time cut of up to 3% in the MDCAT pass percentage (MBBS 52%, BDS 47%), but only to fill seats still vacant in session 2025-26 after every eligible candidate had been considered, with 15 April 2026 as the last date (PM&DC notification No. PF.1-C/2026/Council/Notification/1108). The Punjab policy for session 2026-27 is back at 55% for MBBS and 50% for BDS.

Overseas Pakistani and dual national seats

Candidates on overseas Pakistani or dual national (expatriate) seats may use MDCAT or, instead, SAT-II with at least 550 in Biology, Chemistry and Physics or Mathematics, or the international MCAT or UCAT with 50% marks in the required subjects. They also need at least 60% in their 12th-grade qualification with an IBCC equivalence (PM&DC regulation 8 and the UHS notice).

Other provinces and NUMS

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KMU), Sindh, Balochistan and NUMS (Armed Forces colleges) publish their own admission policies under the same PM&DC regulations. We have not yet checked their session 2026-27 minimums against an official document, so this page and our calculators use the Punjab (UHS) figures.

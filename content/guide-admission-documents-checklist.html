Five mistakes that get applications rejected

  1. Unverified result cards. UHS wants SSC and HSSC copies verified by your board before you scan them (prospectus 2026-27 p. 50). Get this done in the first days of the application window; boards get crowded.
  2. An expired CNIC or B-Form. UHS does not accept expired identity documents (p. 51). Renew before you apply.
  3. Blurry or oversized scans. UHS rejects unclear files and takes JPG/PNG of up to 500 KB, each side of a CNIC as its own file (p. 53).
  4. The wrong domicile for a quota seat. An under-developed district seat needs that district's domicile, and tribal-area seats need "Tribal Area" printed on it (UHS p. 47).
  5. Missing the joining steps. At UHS, selection alone is not admission: you must pay the college fee, report to the college and upload the stamped joining report by the due date (p. 56).

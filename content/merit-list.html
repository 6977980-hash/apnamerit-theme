How to use last year's closing merit

If your aggregate is above a program's closing merit, you would have been admitted last year. Within about 1.5 points either side is borderline, because merit moves a little every year. Enter your aggregate in the merit predictor to see every program at once, or work it out first with the right aggregate calculator.

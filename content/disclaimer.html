Not an official website

Apna Merit is an independent website. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with any university, college, the University of Health Sciences (UHS), the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC), the Higher Education Commission (HEC) or any government body. University names are used only to identify the institutions whose public data we report.

Information only

Our calculators, merit tables and chance labels are for general information. They are based on official documents, but:

  • universities can change formulas, seats, quotas and deadlines at any time;
  • official documents sometimes contain errors that are corrected later;
  • we may make mistakes when copying figures, despite our checks; and
  • past closing merits do not predict future ones with certainty.

Always confirm with the university

Before applying, paying any fee or making a decision, check the formula, merit lists and dates on the university's official website or admission notice. If our figure and the official figure differ, the official figure is correct, and we would be grateful if you tell us through the contact page.

Predictions are estimates

"Strong", "Borderline" and "Unlikely" are estimates based on the last two years of closing merits, explained in our methodology. They are not a promise of admission or rejection.

How is the IIUI aggregate calculated?

The International Islamic University, Islamabad sets its merit in its official Admission Policy. Most undergraduate programs have no admission test and admit on previous qualification (PQM) only:

IIUI aggregate weights by program type
Program typeSSC (Matric)HSSC (FSc)Admission test
BS on previous qualification (policy 1.6)40%60%None
BS with a mandatory test (policy 1.8)15%25%60%

The test formula applies where the accreditation body (for example PEC for engineering or PBC for law) does not set its own weights. If your HSSC result is awaited, IIUI calculates merit on the marks you obtained in Part-I: enter those with a total of 550.

Worked example

With a 60/100 admission test the same marks give 13.64 + 21.59 + 36.00 = 71.23% on the test formula.

IIUI merit lists and closing merit

IIUI displays first, second and third merit lists program-wise on its website, separately for the male and female campuses. It does not publish a single closing-merit document we could verify, so this page shows the formula only. Compare with Punjab University on the PU merit list or use the PU aggregate calculator, which counts Matric very differently (one quarter of its marks).

Results are rounded as IIUI's policy says: 49.99% stays 49.99%, it is not rounded up to 50%.

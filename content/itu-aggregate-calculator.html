How is the ITU aggregate calculated?

The Information Technology University (ITU), Lahore, lists its selection criteria on its official Norms & Criteria page. For BS Computer Science and BS Artificial Intelligence it is: Matric 15% + Intermediate (or Part-I) 35% + ITU admission test / SAT-I / USAT 50%.

The calculator uses the BS CS / AI formula because it is the only one where ITU states how Matric and Intermediate are split. For the other programs ITU gives a single "academic record" weight without saying how it is formed.

ITU test score needed

You need at least 50% in the ITU Admissions Test for CS, AI, M&T and FinTech (40% for Economics with Data Science). You can skip the ITU test with SAT I (at least 550 in Math and in EBRW) or a USAT-CS, USAT-E or USAT-GS score of 50% or more. Matric and Intermediate must each be at least 50% for BS CS. ITU posts merit lists program by program on its website but no closing-merit summary, so we show the formula only. For Lahore computing seats with official closing merits, see PUCIT on the PU merit list.

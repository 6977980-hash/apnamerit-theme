How is the GIKI aggregate calculated?

GIKI's Comparative Assessment Criteria rank undergraduate applicants by admission test 85% + SSC (Matric) or equivalent 15%. FSc marks are not in the formula; they only decide eligibility (60% each in Mathematics, Physics and overall for engineering and computing).

  • Test share = test score % × 0.85
  • Matric share = Matric marks ÷ Matric total × 15

A-level students use their O-level result (IBCC equivalence) for the 15% part; High School Diploma, IB or DAE holders use their last completed qualification.

Worked example

PartMarksWeightShare
GIKI admission test70%85%59.5000
Matric1000 / 110015%13.6364
Aggregate100%73.14%

Because the test carries 85%, each test percentage point moves your aggregate by 0.85 points, while ten extra Matric marks add only about 0.14.

Does GIKI publish a closing merit?

We have not found an official closing aggregate per program from GIKI, so we do not show one; figures on other websites are estimates. Your test score is by far the biggest part of your GIKI merit.

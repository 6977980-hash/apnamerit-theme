This page shows only BDS (dentistry) closing merit in Punjab: the 4 public dental colleges and the 18 private dental colleges that admit through the University of Health Sciences (UHS), Lahore, in the 2025-26 open-merit selection lists. For MBBS see the MDCAT merit list (public) and the private medical colleges merit list.

Public BDS closing merit 2025

[am_merit_table uni="uhs" program="BDS (public)"]

All four public dental colleges closed within 0.05 points of each other in the 13th (final) list: de' Montmorency College of Dentistry, Lahore and Nishtar Institute of Dentistry, Multan at 93.46%, Di-PMC Faisalabad at 93.43% and Fatima Jinnah Institute of Dental Sciences, Lahore at 93.42%. In the 1st list they closed between 94.33% and 94.58%, so merit fell by about 1 point (0.99 on average) over 13 lists.

Private BDS closing merit 2025

[am_merit_table uni="uhs" program="BDS (private)" list="3rd"]

In the 3rd private BDS list, the latest UHS published for 2025-26, the Dental Section of Sharif Medical & Dental College, Lahore closed highest at 73.13% and Shahida Islam Dental College, Lodhran lowest at 55.87%. 17 of the 18 colleges closed at or below 70%. Private BDS merit dropped by 16.08 points on average from the 1st list (where it ran from 88.12% to 75.00%) to the 3rd.

What aggregate do you need for BDS?

Public BDS seats use the same PMDC aggregate as MBBS: 50% MDCAT, 40% FSc and 10% Matric (formula explained); the minimum MDCAT score for BDS is lower than for MBBS (see MDCAT passing marks). Calculate yours with the MDCAT aggregate calculator. In 2025 a public BDS seat needed about 93.4% by the final list, while every private BDS college in the 3rd list closed below 74%.

When are the 2026-27 BDS lists?

UHS opens 2026-27 public BDS applications on 15 December 2026, with the provisional order of merit on 6 January 2027 and the first selection list on 11 January 2027, per its admission advertisement. Every date is on the UHS MBBS and BDS admission schedule.

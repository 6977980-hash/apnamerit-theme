How is the FAST aggregate calculated?

FAST-NUCES uses two formulas, set on its official eligibility criteria page:

FAST aggregate weights by program group
Program groupAdmission testHSSC (FSc)SSC (Matric)
BS Computing (CS, AI, Data Science, Cyber Security, Software Engineering)50%40%10%
BS Engineering (Civil, Electrical, Computer)33%50%17%

The admission test can be FAST's own test or NTS NAT (NAT-IE for engineering, NAT-ICS or NAT-IE for computing). Enter your test score as a percentage, or type your marks and change the total. For HSSC, FAST uses Part I and II marks, or Part I alone if Part II is not out when the merit list is compiled.

What is the FAST closing merit?

FAST does not publish program-wise closing merits; its eligibility page says merit cut-off marks are determined by the university. Figures you see on other websites are estimates, so we do not show them. Eligibility is 60% in SSC and 60% in HSSC for engineering, or 50% in HSSC for computing.

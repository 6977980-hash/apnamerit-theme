Which UET list is the final one?

For Fall 2026 UET Lahore published Merit Lists No. 1 to 4 (the PDFs are dated 24 July, 7 August, 13 August and 20 August 2026), then a left-over seats list (8 September) and waiting-queue lists No. 7 to 12 (PDFs dated 17 September to 12 October 2026) for seats that were still vacant. Up to the 4th list UET moves students to a higher preference automatically, so the 4th list is the complete closing merit and is our default. The 1st list and the left-over seats list are in the filter; the 2nd and 3rd lists and the small waiting-queue lists are on UET's downloads page.

For Fall 2025 UET published the Fourth Merit List, then walk-in admissions and two change-of-preference lists; all four are in the filter (year 2025). UET's new admission portal no longer hosts those Fall 2025 files, so they link to their web.archive.org copies.

What do A1, A2 and the other categories mean?

From the UET Undergraduate Prospectus Fall 2026 (pages 22-25): A1 open merit seats for Punjab domicile with subsidized fee; A2 open merit with partly subsidized fee; -Med the same for FSc Pre-Medical students; AP1, AP2 (and AP1-M, AP2-M) open merit for candidates from provinces other than Punjab; L listed districts of South, Central and North Punjab; M children of UET employees; N children of engineers, architects and planners; NM non-Muslims; O children of UET alumni; P B.Tech / BSc Engineering Technology holders; R Layyah and Bhakkar; S children of overseas Pakistanis; T disabled candidates.

What aggregate is needed for UET Lahore?

If your aggregate is above a program's closing figure in your category, you were in range for it in Fall 2026; within about 1.5 points either side is borderline (our method). Calculate yours with the UET aggregate calculator (33% ECAT, 50% FSc Part-I, 17% Matric), which lists the programs your aggregate cleared.

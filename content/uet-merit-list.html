Which UET list is the final one?

UET Lahore published four minimum-aggregate lists for Fall 2025: the Fourth Merit List, then walk-in admissions (2-4 September 2025) and two change-of-preference lists (manual form and online). The later lists only cover programs where seats were still open or students moved, so the Fourth Merit List is the complete one and is our default. Pick another list in the filter to see later figures.

What do A1, A2 and the other categories mean?

From the UET prospectus (pages 21-24): A1 open merit seats for Punjab domicile with subsidized fee; A2 open merit with partly subsidized fee; -Med the same for FSc Pre-Medical students; AP all Pakistan; L listed districts of South, Central and North Punjab; M children of UET employees; N children of engineers, architects and planners; NM non-Muslims; O children of UET alumni; P B.Tech / BSc Engineering Technology holders; R Layyah and Bhakkar; S children of overseas Pakistanis.

What aggregate is needed for UET Lahore?

If your aggregate is above a program's closing figure in your category, you were in range for it in Fall 2025; within about 1.5 points either side is borderline (our method). Calculate yours with the UET aggregate calculator (33% ECAT, 50% FSc Part-I, 17% Matric), which lists the programs your aggregate cleared.

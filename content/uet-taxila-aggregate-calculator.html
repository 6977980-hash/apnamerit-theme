How is the UET Taxila aggregate calculated?

UET Taxila ranks applicants for BSc Engineering and BS Computer Science by a weighted percentage: entry test (ECAT or TCAT) 33% + FSc Part-I (HSSC 1st year) 50% + Matric (SSC) 17%. The weights are listed on the university's admission eligibility page and in section 24.11 of the UG Prospectus 2025.

  • Test share = entry test marks ÷ test total × 33
  • FSc share = (FSc Part-I marks + Hafiz-e-Quran / NCC marks) ÷ Part-I total × 50
  • Matric share = Matric marks ÷ Matric total × 17

The calculator starts with the totals UET Taxila's own calculator uses: test out of 400 and Matric out of 1100. FSc Part-I is usually out of 550; change it if your board's total is different. BS Mathematics and BS Physics have no entry test and use 70% FSc Part-I + 30% Matric (pick that formula in the box above).

What aggregate do you need for UET Taxila?

For 2024 entry, the open-merit closing aggregates ran from 79.435% (Software Engineering) down to 53.071% (Environmental Engineering). Computer Science closed at 77.979% and Computer Engineering at 70.824%; most core engineering programs closed between 58% and 66%. These are last-admitted figures from the prospectus table "Merit Position Entry – 2024"; this year's merit depends on this year's applicants and seats.

Which FSc marks does UET Taxila use?

The merit formula uses FSc Part-I (first-year) marks, because Part-II results come after the merit lists start. Candidates with a DAE use their diploma marks in place of FSc, and the prospectus adds NCC and Hifz-e-Quran marks to the FSc part only for merit, never for eligibility.

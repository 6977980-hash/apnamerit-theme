How is the IST aggregate calculated?

IST's Merit Determination Criteria use two formulas for FSc students:

IST merit formula by program group
Program groupMatricFSc Part-I / DAEEntry test
Engineering (FSc / DAE)20%40%40%
Engineering (A-Level)40%-60%
Computing / Science (FSc)40%60%No test
Computing / Science (A-Level)100%-No test

Engineering means Aerospace, Electrical, Metallurgy and Materials, Mechanical and Avionics Engineering. Computer Engineering, Computer Science, AI, Data Science, Software Engineering, Space Science, Physics, Mathematics, Biotechnology, Chemistry and Remote Sensing & GIS need no entry test and use the 40/60 formula. A-Level students use their IBCC equivalence for the Matric part.

Which entry test does IST accept?

IST does not hold its own test. For the Fall 2026 intake its Entry Test page accepts NTS NAT (NAT-1E or NAT-1CS, July 2025 to July 2026), HEC USAT (USAT-E or USAT-CS), NUST NET (Engineering or CS), UET ECAT, UET Peshawar ETEA, the MUET and NED entry tests and the ACT composite score. You may use your best score. Enter it in the calculator as marks with that test's total (for example ECAT out of 400, NET out of 200) or as a percentage out of 100.

Seats and eligibility

Seats listed by IST for Fall 2026: Mechanical 120, Aerospace 100, Electrical 100, Metallurgy and Materials 80, Avionics 80, Computer Engineering 50, Computer Science 100, AI 50, Data Science 50, Software Engineering 50, Space Science 50, Physics 40, each Mathematics program 50, Biotechnology 40, Chemistry 40 and Remote Sensing & GIS 40 (General Eligibility). Engineering needs 60% in Matric (Science) and 60% overall in FSc Pre-Engineering, ICS or DAE; Pre-Medical students can apply after an 8-week Mathematics course. CS, AI, Data Science and Software Engineering need 50% overall in HSSC with Mathematics, or Pre-Medical with 50%.

Does IST publish a closing merit?

No. IST shows each applicant's merit status after login on its admission portal and does not publish a program-wise closing aggregate, so we do not show one. Figures on other websites are estimates. Apply early and keep checking your portal account for each merit list.

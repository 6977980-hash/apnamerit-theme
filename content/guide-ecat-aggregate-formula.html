What is the ECAT aggregate formula?

ECAT is the engineering entry test of UET Lahore. Both UET Lahore and UET Taxila turn it into an aggregate with the same weights: 33% ECAT + 50% FSc Part-I (first year) + 17% Matric. UET Lahore sets this on page 20 of its Undergraduate Prospectus Fall 2026; UET Taxila lists it on its admission eligibility page and accepts ECAT or its own test (TCAT).

Both universities use first-year marks even if you have finished FSc, because Part-II results come after the merit lists start. Add the three shares to get your aggregate out of 100. The UET Lahore calculator and the UET Taxila calculator do this for you.

Which part of the formula matters most?

One ECAT mark is worth 0.0825 aggregate points (33 ÷ 400). One FSc Part-I mark out of 550 is worth about 0.091 points (50 ÷ 550), and one Matric mark about 0.015 points (17 ÷ 1100). FSc Part-I carries the biggest weight, but it is fixed by the time you sit ECAT, so ECAT is the part you can still move: 40 more ECAT marks add 3.3 points, more than the Fall 2026 gap between Computer Engineering (82.11%) and Data Science (84.02%) at UET Lahore's main campus.

Hafiz-e-Quran and other extra marks

Both UETs add Hafiz-e-Quran marks to the FSc part, never to ECAT. UET Taxila adds 20 marks to FSc Part-I (its prospectus example: 300/400 test, 500 + 20/550 FSc Part-I and 700/1100 Matric give 82.84%). At UET Lahore a verification committee awards 0 to 20 marks after a test, so the exact figure is only known after it.

Common mistakes

  • Using full FSc marks (out of 1100) in the FSc part. The formula uses Part-I, out of your board's Part-I total.
  • Entering ECAT as a percentage. Enter the raw score out of 400.
  • Rounding too early. UET Lahore's lists print aggregates to many decimals, and programs can close within 0.1 points of each other.

How is the PU aggregate calculated?

Punjab University's basic criteria for BS (4-year) programs are printed on page 1 of its official Merit of Last Year 2025 document. Matric counts at one quarter of its marks and is added to your full FA/FSc marks:

PU merit = (Matric ÷ 4 + FA/FSc + additional marks) ÷ (Matric total ÷ 4 + FA/FSc total) × 100

With the usual 1100 + 1100 totals, that works out to Matric 20% and FSc 80%. Programs that hold an entry test use PU's own basic merit calculator formula: the same academic share counts 75% and the test 25%.

What are additional marks at PU?

The formula adds "additional marks" (for example for Hafiz-e-Quran) to your marks before dividing. PU's merit document points to its Admission Regulations for how many marks apply, and departments can make further additions or subtractions before they display merit lists. That is why this calculator gives the basic merit only; add any additional marks to your FSc marks yourself if they apply to you.

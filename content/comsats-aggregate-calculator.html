How is the COMSATS aggregate calculated?

COMSATS University Islamabad (CUI) admits students on open merit with one weightage for engineering and non-engineering bachelor's programs, set on page 44 of its UG Prospectus 2024-25 (the latest undergraduate prospectus on COMSATS' own server):

COMSATS merit weightage (bachelor's programs)
PartWeight
NTS test (NAT)50%
Intermediate (or equivalent)40%
Matriculation (or equivalent)10%

Engineering programs also need a valid NTS score of at least 33% (Pakistan Engineering Council policy). Enter your NTS score as a percentage; if your result shows marks, type the marks and change the total.

This is the same split FAST uses for its computing programs, so the same marks give the same aggregate on our FAST aggregate calculator.

Architecture and design programs

B. Design, B. Fine Arts and Bachelor of Interior Design use 30% past academics and NTS (Matric 3%, Intermediate 12%, NTS 15%) plus 70% creative aptitude test. B. Arch uses Matric/O level 5%, Intermediate/A level 10%, NTS 10%, departmental creative aptitude test 40% and interview 35%. This calculator covers the standard 10/40/50 formula only.

Does COMSATS publish a closing merit?

Each COMSATS campus displays its merit lists on the admission portal and campus notice boards. We have not yet been able to read an official closing merit for any campus, so we do not show one; we will add it only from an official COMSATS list.

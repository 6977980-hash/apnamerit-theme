COMSATS University Islamabad publishes closing merits campus by campus and intake by intake. This page holds the official figures we could read on 31 October 2026: the Lahore campus Fall 2026 merit page (closing aggregates of the 2nd merit list, after which Lahore announced no further lists) and the Islamabad campus Spring 2025 final closing merits. Islamabad also posted Fall 2026 3rd-list closing merits (for example Cyber Security 84.34%, Software Engineering 84.03%, Computer Engineering 78.87%); pick the list "3rd" in the filter to see them, or the year 2025 for Islamabad Spring 2025. For an older Fall comparison, the year 2023 shows Islamabad's Fall 2023 2nd closing merit (Computer Science 87.10%, Software Engineering 85.42%, AI 85.33%).

What aggregate is needed for COMSATS?

For Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, Data Science and Cyber Security, the official figures run from 82% (Lahore) to 88.26% (Islamabad); every other program closed between 50.07% and 78.01%. Spring intakes have fewer seats and applicants than Fall, so treat Islamabad Spring figures as a guide for Fall, not an exact match. Calculate yours with the COMSATS aggregate calculator (50% NTS NAT, 40% Intermediate, 10% Matric), which lists the programs your aggregate cleared.

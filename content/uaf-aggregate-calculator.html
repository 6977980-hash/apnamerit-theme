How is the UAF aggregate calculated?

The University of Agriculture, Faisalabad admits undergraduates on three parts, as its official admissions advertisement 2026-27 states: Matric 30%, Intermediate Part-I (first year) 30% and the UAF entry test 40%. Admission is first given on this basis, before the Intermediate Part-II result, and becomes final once your Intermediate result meets the program's minimum eligibility.

What is the UAF entry test passing score?

The entry test is compulsory for every undergraduate program. According to the advertisement you need at least 33% for programs of the Faculty of Agricultural Engineering & Technology and 50% for Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) and Pharm-D; other programs have no minimum test score. UAF held two entry tests for 2026-27, on 5 July and 2 August 2026.

UAF merit lists and closing merit

UAF posts a separate PDF for every merit list of every program and campus (main campus, the Toba Tek Singh, Burewala, Depalpur and Narowal sub-campuses and the constituent colleges) on its merit lists page. It does not publish a summary of closing merits, so this page shows the formula only.
